Your business (printed on the invoice)
Invoice numbers follow PREFIX/SEQ/FY, e.g. SMT/49/2026-27. The sequence restarts from the value above each financial year; the app proposes the next free number automatically.
PDF font

To print invoices in Bahnschrift, choose bahnschrift.ttf from C:\Windows\Fonts on a Windows PC (copy it to Desktop first if the picker can't open that folder). It is saved once in your private database and used for PDFs on every phone and PC. Without it, the built-in Barlow font (a close look-alike) is used.

Users

Everyone signs in with their own email. Users can raise and edit invoices and parties; admins can also change settings, manage users, delete invoices and restore backups. Disable an account to block access without deleting it.

Bring in your Excel data

All 42 invoices from the SMT_Bill_Format workbook (up to SMT/48/2026-27) are already loaded. Use this only to bring in a newer copy of the workbook: the app reads the Master sheet and adds every invoice, party and item; invoices with the same number are replaced.

Backup & export

Save a backup regularly (to Google Drive or email) — it is also the way to move the data anywhere else.
